Student Hostel Booking
Problem Statement

Every semester, students hunting for university accommodation rely on printed hostel lists, word-of-mouth, or a slow desktop web page to find a room. It's hard to compare rooms side by side, prices and availability go stale, and there's no clear confirmation once a booking is made — leaving students unsure whether they actually have a room or whether payment went through. This causes duplicate bookings, missed deadlines, and last-minute scrambling closer to term start.

This project designs a mobile-first Hostel Booking app that lets students browse hostels and rooms, compare options side by side, select and confirm a booking, and always know their booking/payment status at a glance.

Objectives
Let students browse and compare available hostels/rooms quickly on a phone.
Make the booking flow fast, clear, and mistake-resistant (no accidental double-bookings).
Give constant visibility into booking and payment status after confirmation.
Apply mobile-appropriate form design, feedback, and visual hierarchy throughout.
Validate the design with real user research and usability testing, then iterate.
Target Users

Undergraduate and postgraduate students who:

Need to secure accommodation before a term deadline, often under time pressure.
Are comparing multiple hostels/room types on price, distance, and amenities.
Range from students booking for the first time (need guidance/reassurance) to returning students who just want to rebook fast.

Main Features
Browse Hostels & Rooms — list/filter hostels, view room types, photos, pricing, availability.
Compare Rooms — side-by-side comparison of selected room options.
Select a Room — choose a specific room/bed space.
Confirm Booking — review details, enter required info, confirm.
Booking & Payment Status — always-visible status (pending / confirmed / paid) with next steps.
Project Summary

Over three weeks the team will: (1) conduct user research and define personas, requirements, and task flows; (2) design and link 12+ mobile screens in Balsamiq covering at least 3 complete user scenarios (browse-and-compare, book-a-room, check-status); (3) run usability testing with 5+ representative users, document findings, and revise the prototype accordingly.
